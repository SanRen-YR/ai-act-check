Frequently Asked Questions

Isn't there a free official EU AI Act checker?

Yes — the official EU Compliance Checker is excellent for deep legal review. But it takes 30+ minutes, uses complex legal language, and only tells you your risk category. Our tool is built for busy SME founders who want a quick, plain-English answer and a clear action plan.

Is this a legal certification or audit?

No. This is a preliminary compliance self-assessment tool. It helps you understand your starting point and what you need to prepare. For formal legal compliance and certification, you must consult a qualified EU lawyer or notified body.

When should I upgrade to the €99 Professional Review?

If your AI system is classified as High Risk, or if you're preparing for EU market entry and need a more detailed gap analysis with prioritized next steps, the Professional Review is recommended.

What happens to my data?

We only use your email to send you the report you requested. We do not share your information with third parties. Your questionnaire answers are used solely to generate your assessment.
