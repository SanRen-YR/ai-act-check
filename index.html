Risk levels

The four tiers of the AI Act

Which tier your AI falls into decides which obligations apply — and how urgent they are.

Unacceptable risk
Banned since Feb 2025
Manipulative or deceptive AI, social scoring, exploitation of vulnerable people, untargeted facial-image scraping, and more. Using these is illegal, full stop — fines up to €35M or 7% of turnover.
High risk
Obligations from 2 Dec 2027
AI used in recruitment, credit decisions, education, critical infrastructure and similar. Requires conformity assessment, technical documentation, data quality, logging, human oversight, and EU database registration.
Limited risk
Transparency duties — now
Chatbots, virtual assistants and AI-generated content must be disclosed: tell users they're talking to AI, and label deepfakes and synthetic content (content-marking transition ends 2 Dec 2026).
Minimal risk
No extra AI Act duties
Most everyday AI — spam filters, product recommendations, basic automation. Other EU law (like GDPR) may still apply to the data involved.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this legal advice?
No. This tool performs a structured screening based on the AI Act and points you to the obligations that likely apply. It is not a legal opinion, and it can't replace a qualified lawyer for high-risk or borderline cases.
Our company is not in the EU. Does this apply to us?
Yes, in most cases. The AI Act applies to any provider or deployer — wherever they are — whose AI system is placed on the EU market or used by people in the EU. That's exactly the situation of most cross-border e-commerce and AI companies.
We build on OpenAI's API. Are we a "GPAI provider"?
Usually not. Calling a general-purpose model through an API generally makes you a provider or deployer of an AI system, not of the model itself. The questionnaire handles this distinction and tells you which side you're on.
What about GDPR?
The AI Act and GDPR overlap but are separate laws. This check focuses on the AI Act and flags obvious GDPR items (like a privacy notice) where relevant — but it is not a GDPR audit. If you process EU personal data, get a GDPR review separately.
What happens to my answers?
Nothing. Everything runs in your browser. You can close the tab and it's gone. When you export the report, the file is generated locally on your device.
How accurate is the risk score?
The score reflects how many obligations likely apply to you and how prepared you look, based on standard AI Act interpretation. It's a prioritisation aid, not a certification. For high-risk or unclear cases, we recommend a short consultation with a qualified professional.
